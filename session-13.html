Reach × turn, and one hinge for two banks

Read this session: a count written as a two-digit numeral; addition lifted to area and proportion priced as a defect; the hinge built, corrected into five pieces, put in perspective and tilted; the Minkowski naming settled; one diagram for a decade's thinking; the double-gauged rectangle resolved and given its floor

Four days, one argument, the longest run in the record, filed the day it closed. Every plate below began as one of the reader's sketches, and every one passed his iPad check before filing.

Landed The count and the panels · 21–22 September

n of n (709) built the reach × turn sketch: a pencil from O on a line marked 0..n, a projector that forces the free horizontal at 1/n, turn families shifting the fine marks cell by cell — n of n = n² read as a two-digit base-n numeral, turn digit and reach digit, the shared boundary marks as the carry. The next day's sketch sheet was read in the law/gauge/defect register: the apex lifts addition to area without defect; the diagonal tests proportion, with ad − bc as the determinant in area currency. Additivity is the law; proportion is the event. Law, gauge, defect (20) draws it against an incommensurable law: the diagonal of a square read against a counted base, Reach carried across a Turn.

Landed The hinge · 22–23 September

The hinge, seven versions in two days. The trapezoid's diagonals meet at X, height c/(b + c), bisecting the harmonic-mean chord; two findings verified — 2/HM = 1/b + 1/c, and each sliver D = h·HM/4. The reader read the red quadrilateral as the perspective of a square, and it was: the rail a horizon, VD the fourth harmonic of infinity, a homography reconstructing the plan square at every drag. Then his corrections, recorded in their order: C is meant to represent an area; C is exactly A + B, not A + B + D; and the final ruling, A, D, B, D, C as five disjoint regions tiling the rectangle — “no overlap, except in the interpretation.” His last question tilted the top rail: a fourth vanishing point arrives, the four stay harmonic with 1/OVD + 1/OVD′ = 2/c, and the affine layers park rather than draw falsely.

Answered The Minkowski naming · 22 September

The reader's semicircle atop the two Pythagorases asked whether his lower identity deserved the name “Minkowski Pythagorean theorem,” and VI.31 for uv (2371) settled it on the claim's own terms. The Minkowski circle on the diameter is a rectangular hyperbola whose Q-right apex sits at the hinge height; the half-square on the diagonal is the triangle verbatim; one null slide delivers the other two; the negative square crossing the equals sign is the identity as VI.31 for the product form.

Found One hinge, two banks · 23 September

Brought together at the reader's request, one hinge, two banks (2934) found that the juxtaposed A + B = C is the signed uv-Pythagoras on O–M — one theorem, two proofs, trade against tiling as signature (1, 1) against positive-definite. The reader: “this one diagram encapsulates a lot of what I had been thinking about for over a decade.” Tilt sorted the claims into three strata — rail-borne park, form-borne stand unchanged, incidence-borne stand and grow. His red-line progression proved harmonic: the bounce x → c²/(2c − x), parabolic, reciprocal gaps in arithmetic step. The check refused its author twenty-four times on the claim that tilt would break the arithmetic law; tilt does not, and only the step's value 1/c is level furniture. The refusal is filed in the plate.

Landed The rate loop · 24 September

The reader's unresolved double-gauged rectangle — widths ka, kb, heights jc, jd — resolved as two laws sharing the letters: the round trip kj = 1, true per channel always; and one common rate, true exactly on the diagonal, ad = bc. Read against The law and the gauge, it is the congruence square's ground floor: the rate loop (2175) unfolds one point of that square into its rectangle. The rate square itself was found where Session 7 left it, and the two pages were found to draw different seams for different laws: straight-iff-seam upstairs, gilding the pushbroom edges; common-rate-iff-diagonal on the ground floor. The stair lands diagonal to diagonal. Repair, same day: Rate square 3D gained its two ground-floor links; script byte-identical; its own check 27/27.

Seasoning Two additions to the shelf

The reciprocal register as one currency across both estates: the camera's weld written in reciprocals, 1/μ = −1/λ − r, has the normal form of the bounce's 1/g′ = 1/g + 1/c, the hinge's 2/HM = 1/b + 1/c, and the tilt's 1/OVD + 1/OVD′ = 2/c — the rate loop proposed as the teller's window between them. Priced: observation, not theorem. And a recurring shape, three times in two sessions: orthogonality the law and congruence the event; additivity the law and proportion the event; the round trip the law and the common rate the event.
