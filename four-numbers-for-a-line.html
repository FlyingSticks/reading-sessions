Four Numbers for a Line

A straight line in space is named by four numbers measured against one fixed card: two for where the line pierces the card, two for which way the line points.

Two + two = four numbers

ACROSS−1
UP−1
ALTITUDE α35°
BEARING β40°

These four numbers name the green line and no other line in space.

Conventions

  • One card. Panels 2 and 3 show the same card in the same place. All four numbers are measured against it.
  • The card need not be square to the lines. Here it is not: if it were, α and β would both be 0.
  • A line has no arrowhead. The pairs (α, β) and (−α, β + 180°) describe the same family. Keeping β between −90° and +90° gives each family exactly one pair.
  • One exception. A family lying parallel to the card never pierces it, so it has no piercing spot and no four numbers on this card.
  • What is convention and what is not. The zero mark, the unit of length, the turn of the card and the card itself are choices. Changing them changes the numbers. It does not move the line, and it does not change the count: four.

Terms

card
A flat reference surface, fixed once, that the lines pierce.
zero mark O
The spot on the card counted as (0, 0).
across, up
The two ruled directions on the card; x and y.
piercing spot
The one spot where a given line passes through the card.
x′, y′, z′
Lines through the piercing spot: x′ parallel to across, y′ parallel to up, z′ square to the card.
level plane
The plane through x′ and z′.
plumb line
A line parallel to y′, used to drop a point onto the level plane.
shadow
The line in the level plane directly beneath the green line; its plan projection.
bearing β
The angle in the level plane from z′ (β = 0) to the shadow.
altitude α
The upright angle from the shadow (α = 0) to the line.
