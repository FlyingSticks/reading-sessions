Complex product as a compass construction

Multiplying by w = (a, b) carries the frame {1, i} to {w, i·w}. The coordinates (c, d) of z are then counted along the new frame: swing radius c about O onto the line Ow, swing radius d onto the line O(i·w), and close the rectangle. Its far corner is w·z.
