Every word the primer's plates define, set beside the book's word for the same thing, where the book has one, and the standard name under which it can be looked up elsewhere. Book words the primer has not reached

the book's wordin one sentence, paraphrased from the book's glossary
ReadingA congruence given a role: what the lines are (edges or rays) together with a graduation along them. The second half of the title.
GlideThe 1984 drawing construction: the congruence read as edges.
Cross-slitThe optical construction: the congruence read as rays through two slits at two depths. Plate 2 reaches the family of rays, but not the graduation.
Rate squareThe space of readings, one point for each pair of channel rates. Plate 3 touches one corner of it, the pushbroom.

The book's glossary has about sixty entries. Only those the plates touch, and these four, are listed here.

Status. The book's words were taken from its live glossary on 18 November 2026, and each links to its entry there. The standard names are the usual ones in line geometry, projective geometry and the theory of permutations; they have not been audited outside. Where a plain word and a book word are set side by side, the claim is only that they name the same thing, or, where the cell says "compare", that they are near neighbours and not the same.
