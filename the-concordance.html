Book words the primer has not reached

the book's wordin one sentence, paraphrased from the book's glossary
ReadingA congruence given a role: what the lines are (edges or rays) together with a graduation along them. The second half of the title.
GlideThe 1984 drawing construction: the congruence read as edges.
Cross-slitThe optical construction: the congruence read as rays through two slits at two depths. Plate 2 reaches the family of rays, but not the graduation.
Rate squareThe space of readings, one point for each pair of channel rates. Plate 3 touches one of its two pushbroom corners.

The book's glossary has about sixty entries. Only those the plates touch, and these four, are listed here.

Status. The book's words were taken from its live glossary on 18 November 2026, and each links to its entry there. The standard names were audited outside on 19 November 2026 and nine were amended. The rows for plate 6 ("One, then another") were added afterwards and are unaudited. For the rest, the book's column was audited outside the same day against seventeen glossary entries quoted word for word, and ten cells were amended. The auditor saw those entries only, not the book's chapters. Where a plain word and a book word are set side by side with no qualification, the claim is that they name the same thing. Where the cell says "compare" or "corresponds to", they are near neighbours or matched one for one, and not the same thing.
