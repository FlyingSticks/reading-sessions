The dial

One parameter, two laws: the exchange identity and the trichotomy read the same d

Theorem 0's Turn gauge has a single free parameter once the step unit is chosen: the dial d = D/u. This page shows that the dial is read simultaneously by two meters. The exchange meter reads the dimensionless product of Theorem 0, confined to [1, 2). The trichotomy meter reads the gauge's position in the Möbius trichotomy — how far its conjugacy class sits from the parabolic boundary where Reach lives. Both readings are functions of d alone, so eliminating d links them in closed form: the two meters are welded.

The excess.  ε ≡ tr²Mdet M − 4  =  (L − D)²L D  =  1d(1 + d)  > 0  for every d > 0. The exchange.  P ≡ |Δ²y| · L(Δy₀)²  =  2(1 + d)2 + d  ∈ [1, 2). The weld. Eliminating d:  ε = (2 − P)²2 P(P − 1).  The trichotomy position is a function of the exchange product, and conversely.

Reading the instrument

At the compression end the marks pile against the crimson ring, the exchange needle sits on its closed bracket at the floor P = 1, and the trichotomy dot flies off toward the open end of the hyperbolic ray: maximal compression is maximally hyperbolic. Turn the dial the other way and the marks spread toward equal steps, the needle climbs toward its open parenthesis at 2, and the dot slides in toward the gold parabolic line — Reach's own address — without ever landing on it. The two meters cannot be moved separately. That is the weld formula in the statement box, verified numerically in the check script at every sampled d.

The gold points on the ruler are the gauge's own fixed points: the origin and the first mark. Their multipliers are exact reciprocals — the repulsion from 0 is precisely the attraction into u — and the harmonic crowding of the marks is nothing but hyperbolic flow into an attracting fixed point, read on an affine ruler.

Why the bare discriminant will not do

An element of PGL(2, R) is a matrix only up to a scalar: M and kM are the same projective map. Under M → kM the trace scales by k and the determinant by k², so the bare discriminant tr² − 4 det scales by k² — it is a property of the matrix you happened to write, not of the map. The ratio tr²/det survives the rescaling, and its excess over the parabolic threshold 4 is the honest conjugacy reading. For the Turn matrix M = [[L, 0], [1, D]] the excess computes to (L−D)²/LD = 1/(d(1+d)), strictly positive for every finite d: hyperbolic throughout, with the earlier claim “discriminant = u²” now recognized as the unit-dependent shadow of this invariant.

The two limits, on both meters at once

d → 0 (maximal compression). P → 1, the floor, attained; ε → ∞. Infinity arrives at the first mark and the gauge's hyperbolic character is at its most violent — multiplier (1+d)/d at the origin blowing up.

d → ∞ (affine recovery). P → 2, the open ceiling; ε → 0. The gauge sheds its hyperbolicity and slides toward the parabolic boundary of the trichotomy — the conjugacy class where Reach sits — reaching it only in the degenerate limit where the Turn family ends and the ring leaves the page. The half-open interval [1, 2) and the half-open ray (0, ∞] are the same half-openness, seen on two meters.
