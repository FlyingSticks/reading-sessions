The Reading Sessions

What the book yields when it is read, kept apart until it earns its way in

The book was written in two days and is being read at leisure. These pages are the record of that reading: one page per session, each holding what the session produced — repairs landed in the book, observations the book prompted, and sentences that may one day belong to it. Nothing here is absorbed into the book on the day it is written. A yield sits in this record until it has been walked past a few times and is still standing; only then does it move, by a deliberate revision, into the chapters. The book states; this record collects.
