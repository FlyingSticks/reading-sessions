The Defect Census

Every defect-like quantity in the book and the sessions — its space, its currency, its zero set, and what it obeys

The book never defines defect, and it turns out it never needed the word to use the thing. Chapter 2 bounds one, Chapter 3 coordinatizes one, Chapter 6 removes the removable part of one in closed form and names the remainder. The sessions then priced two more and, this week, watched a third refuse to clear against every counted gauge. This page is the audit that should precede any fold-in: a census of the quantities, one entry each, so that the genus can be named without manufacturing a homonym. The admission rule for a row: it prices a failed agreement. A verdict that merely compares a price against a resource is listed, but flagged as a verdict.

The genus, proposed

A defect is the measured amount by which a required agreement fails.

Every defect lives in a definite space and is quoted in a currency; re-denomination moves it between currencies but neither creates nor destroys it. Its zero set is where the agreement holds — identically on a locus, at isolated relocatable points, or nowhere at all. Some defects obey a floor, some a conservation. The shared grammar is the conservation page’s: a total fixed by the law before any choice, a policy that relocates it, and zero available only on a locus, never by arrangement.

The map

What the rows amount to. Solid edges are re-denominations — one quantity changing currency, zero set carried intact. The dashed tie is an analogy of shape between two different species, and the struck tie is session 4’s finding: a resemblance that was tested and failed. Tap a node to open its row.

One genus, several species. The miss family is genuinely one defect in four currencies — its zero set, the seam, travels with it through every re-denomination. The refusal and the ledger are different species with the same shape: two currencies each, and no way to be small in both. The account is alone in its space, conserved rather than floored. The crossing is the census’s cautionary tale: it looked like the cubic’s zero and is not.

Two refusals, one shape

The census’s dashed tie, made operable. Left: the ledger — frac(nL) for gauges n = 1…24; for √2 it never posts a zero, for 7⁄5 it clears on every fifth gauge. Right: Theorem 0’s floor — the witness product 2(1+d)/(2+d) never dips below 1, touching it only at the unreachable d = 0. Different species (law against gauge; demand against demand), one grammar: the defect can be traded between currencies, and there is a refusal underneath that no trade clears.

Tap a bar to select its gauge. Gold rings on the ledger are posted zeros — √2 has none at any gauge; a rational law posts them on schedule. The floor curve starts at 1 exactly and climbs toward 2: relieve the equal-step defect and the mark for infinity pays, and their product never goes under the floor.

Findings

Four families and two solos. The miss family (C2–C5) is one defect: the seam is its zero set in every currency, which is why naming the genus costs the book nothing there — the chapters were already pricing the same disagreement in ray, algebraic, metric and drawn units. The refusal (C1) and the ledger (C10) are distinct species joined only by shape. The account (C8) is its own family: conserved, not floored, and living in a space no other row shares — the ruling that A is not Δ holds and generalizes. The cubic (C9) prices the rig; its impostor, the miniature’s crossing, is the census’s standing reminder that not every zero is a seam. The bias (C6) is the working example of what a named genus buys: an accounting method — identify the defect, remove its rational part in closed form, name the remainder. The budget (C7) is a verdict built on a defect, listed here with its open gap showing.

The porch, it turns out, promised the word before the sessions found it. The calendar’s leftover — movable, never removable — is a ledger of species C10. The map case prices curvature by what classical geometry has always called the angle defect: the genus name is old, and the book’s middle quarrel already used it. The canvas quarrel forced Theorem 0, which is C1. All three of the porch’s quarrels were defect stories; the census only says so out loud.

One discipline carries over whole from the conservation page: species are kept apart by their spaces. Law-against-gauge is not reading-against-reading; count-versus-placement is not the rate square; an isolated zero is not a locus. The genus earns its name only if every use of the word can answer the census’s five questions — which space, which currency, which zero set, floor or conservation, whose quarrel.

The fold-in, drafted and held

Per the record’s contract, nothing below is folded. Three pieces, smallest first; the third waits on an audit.

Draft · glossary entry, the genus

defect — the measured amount by which a required agreement fails. A defect lives in a definite space and is quoted in a currency; re-denomination trades it between currencies but neither creates nor destroys it. Its zero set is where the agreement holds: identically on a locus (the seam), at isolated relocatable points (a crossing), or nowhere (an incommensurable law against a counted gauge). Some defects obey a floor (Theorem 0), some a conservation (the account). Local names in this book: λ − μ, N(δ), the gap, the bow, the bias.

Draft · one paragraph after Theorem 0, chapter 2

Theorem 0’s product is the first appearance of a quantity this book will meet under several names. The equal-step defect and the finite mark for infinity are two currencies of one refusal, and the floor says that no re-denomination clears it. When Chapter 3 writes λ − μ and Chapter 6 prices a bias, they are pricing the same kind of thing: the measured amount by which a required agreement fails. Agreement, wherever this book finds it, is the zero of such a measure.

Held · the seam’s second definition

The seam is the zero set of the defect λ − μ. On the seasoning shelf since 3 September, unaudited. Not to be folded before the reading is checked against the seam theorem in full; session 4 discharged the one-rate-zero edge (silence, not collision), which is consistent but not the whole audit.

Terms this page adds

One line each, tagged as the conservation page tags them: sessions is coinage of these pages.

genussessions
The concept over the local names: defect as such, prior to its species.
speciessessions
A defect classified by whose quarrel it prices: demand/demand, reading/reading, law/gauge, anchoring/anchoring, estimate/truth.
spacesessions
Where a defect lives; species are kept apart by their spaces before anything else.
currencysessions
The unit a defect is quoted in within its space; one defect may hold several.
re-denominationsessions
Trading a defect between currencies; carries the zero set intact, creates and destroys nothing.
zero setsessions
Where the agreement holds — a locus, isolated points, or nowhere.
floor / conservationsessions
The two refusals a defect may obey: a bound it cannot go under, or a total no policy can change.
