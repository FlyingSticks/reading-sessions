How It Goes Together

A map of three bodies of work and the joins between them. 19 November 2026; revised the same day when primer plate 7 was built; revised 20 November 2026 (the circle, the opening paragraph, the caption of figure 1, the rows J2 and J4, two entries under Terms, and a note on the reading of J1 to J5); revised 21 November 2026 after the outside audit of primer plates 6 and 7 (rows J4 and J6, the standing of the plates, and the entry "involution" under Terms). This map itself is not audited outside. The file names assume this page sits in the same folder as the plates.

There are three bodies of work: the book, the primer that stands in front of it, and the mirror thread that runs beside both. One kind of object occurs in all three: the involution. It is not one and the same map in each. In the thread it is a mirror of the plane, in the primer a pairing of dots or of the points of a line, in the book a map of space. The figure shows which joins between them have been shown, which are only a rhyme, and which one was tested and struck.

Terms

involution
A reversible rule that is its own backward rule and is not the identity. Applied twice it returns everything; applied once it moves at least one thing. This is the book's usage: its Involution entry counts three among its six maps, although the entry's first sentence, read alone, does not exclude the identity.
harmonic pairing
The pairing of P with P′ on a line through two fixed points A and B, where (P, P′; A, B) = −1.
cross-ratio −1
The cross-ratio (P, P′; A, B) of a pair of partners P, P′ and the two points A, B that stay on their line, when the pairing is harmonic. It belongs to four points on a line. Primer plate 5, figure 3, computes it.
sign of a map
+1 for an even number of mirrors, −1 for an odd number. It belongs to a whole distance-preserving map, not to a pair of points. It is a different number from the cross-ratio −1.
congruence (book)
A family of lines in space: here, the lines meeting both slits.
congruence (plates)
A distance-preserving map.
shown
Stated on a plate or in a check script and supported there. The note under it says how far the support goes.
rhyme
A likeness of form with no statement that one thing is the other.
struck
Stated, tested, and found false.
open
Not yet stated as a claim.
seam
The book's word for where the drawing reading and the camera reading agree.
