the rig in the round — four quadrants: depth trails and gauge frames

The orbitable rig of folded-rigs-3d, with the picture plane grown to its full four quadrants — the origin at its center is where the slits axis pierces the screen. Two new families live on the plane: depth trails, what one depth line prints under each reading, and gauge frames, what one depth prints. The glide's net is straight and arithmetic; the camera's crowds harmonically toward the origin, and its trails bend. Drag, fold, and fuse as before; the labels chip clears all text when you want the line structure alone.

readings
view — or drag the empty paper to orbit 


  

    
camera reading (cross-slit) — ray scene-point → slit, read at the screen. Mark Xc = X·m; equal depth steps print harmonically. Law and gauge welded by optics.

    
glide reading — ray footprint → slit, read at scene depth, carried home along the level plane. Mark Xg = X/m; equal depth steps print arithmetically (Reach). Gauge free.

    
corner mark x = X — the ⊥ projection, both feet at −∞; the mean proportional between the other two.

    
u, v — the two feet on the slits axis; each channel runs both readings through its own foot. The fold lays the v-plane onto the u-plane.

    
prints on the plane — the two channels' marks assembled into coordinates on the screen: camera print (Xc, Yc), filled, where the 3D ray strikes; glide print (Xg, Yg), open, both marks carried home; ⊥ print (x, y), the corner pair. The dashed square prints to the two rectangles — areas ×(mᵤ·mᵥ) and its reciprocal, product 1.

    
depth trails — the print of the depth line through the point (X, Y held, λ running): the glide trail is straight, an arithmetic march out along the fixed direction (X·r_u, Y·r_v); the camera trail is a hyperbolic arc from the ⊥ print asymptotically into the origin. Fuse the feet and the arc straightens into the perspective star.

    
gauge frames — one square frame per depth (λ = ¼, ½, ¾, 1) printed twice: camera frames nest inward, crowding harmonically; glide frames step outward in equal strides. Level curves of depth on the screen; the trails are their flow lines. Frame shape carries AR = m_v/m_u — squares exactly when the feet fuse.

  






  
What the orbit adds — and what it doesn't touch

  
Nothing in the geometry changed. The fold map sending rig coordinates
  (λ, X, Y) to the sheet is linear at every θ, so straight lines stay
  straight while the v-plane swings, and every incidence the flat plate drew — marks on traces,
  the ray through both slits, the harmonic hinge — survives the orbit untouched. The view is a
  plain orthographic turn of the same object: drafting rules, no vanishing tricks. The old sheet
  is still here as a preset; folded-rigs-v2 remains the full argument, this plate its
  round-the-back companion.


  
The face-on payoff

  
Face the picture plane and the depth axis projects to a single point: both channel planes
  arrive edge-on, each collapsing onto its own trace — you literally watch the
  camera reading and the glide reading deliver
  their marks to the screen from the side, like two T-square blades feeding one drawing. On the
  plane, the marks pair up into prints. The camera print (Xc, Yc) is not
  a construction: it is where the physical ray through both slits strikes the screen — checked to
  the decimal, strike = (X·mᵤ, Y·mᵥ). The
  glide print (Xg, Yg) stands one reciprocal out on each axis, and the
  ⊥ print (x, y) holds the geometric middle of the rectangle they span. The dashed unit square
  printing to two rectangles — camera in, glide out, areas in reciprocal — is the v2 front-view
  inset promoted to the object itself.


  
Four quadrants — the screen in full

  
The picture plane now carries all four quadrants, and its center earns a name: the
  origin is where the slits axis pierces the screen. The two traces through it are the
  plane's axes, marks may change sign, and nothing in the readings prefers the first quadrant —
  negate X and all three u-channel marks negate with it. The origin is also a fixed point of the
  whole instrument: material on the axis prints to the origin under every reading, at every
  depth, for every placement of the feet.


  
New on this plate — depth trails and gauge frames

  
The new information is a pair of nets the screen inherits from depth. Hold X and Y and let λ
  run: the depth line through the point prints twice. The glide trail is
  straight — (Xg, Yg) = (X·(1+λ·r_u), Y·(1+λ·r_v)) is linear in λ, an
  arithmetic march outward along the fixed direction (X·r_u, Y·r_v).
  The camera trail bends —
  (Xc, Yc) = (X/(1+λ·r_u), Y/(1+λ·r_v)) traces the arc of a hyperbola,
  (u−v)·Xc·Yc + v·Y·Xc − u·X·Yc = 0, from the ⊥ print asymptotically
  into the origin. That is the punchline in one sentence: a two-slit camera bends straight
  depth lines; the glide does not. And every camera trail, whatever its (X, Y), runs to the
  same origin — the one vanishing point depth owns on this screen. Fuse the feet and the
  hyperbola degenerates to the straight ray Yc/Xc = Y/X: the familiar
  perspective star is the Δ = 0 member of a family of curves.

  
Crossing the trails run the gauge frames: one square frame per depth, printed twice.
  The camera's frames nest inward, crowding harmonically toward the
  origin; the glide's step outward in equal strides. The frames are the
  level curves of depth on the picture plane and the trails are its flow lines — together they
  are the depth coordinate system the screen has carried all along, drawn out. The frames' shape
  also carries the anisotropy AR = m_v/m_u: rectangles in general,
  squares exactly when the feet fuse.


  
Same instrument, same dials

  
The fold θ still swings between camera country (open 90°, where the forward reading is
  honest optics) and drafting country (flat, where the glide strokes never leave the paper); the
  ray is drawn at every θ as the folded image of itself, honest optics only at 90°. Fuse v → u
  and Δ = r_u·r_v·(r_v − r_u) goes to zero: one denominator, the prints' rectangle squares up,
  and the readings' drawn lines fuse into perspective. The ladders still run in the u channel —
  harmonic in, arithmetic out — and the readout still carries the hinge identity
  Xg·Xc = x² live in both channels.


  
What is checked, what is a reading

  
The constructions on this sheet run under check-folded-rigs-3d-4q.js — 26 numbered
  assertions. The parent plate's are re-run — the ×1 picture plane, the reciprocal pair and geometric progression,
  the harmonic hinge, the print-area product, linearity of the fold map, the ray's incidence
  with both slits and the point, the strike landing exactly on the camera print at every fold
  angle, both ladders, the seam meter's zero set — and the new material adds its own: the origin
  as the fixed point of both readings at all depths and feet; odd symmetry of all marks
  (four-quadrant consistency); the glide trail's collinearity and its direction; the camera
  trail's hyperbola relation, its passage through the ⊥ print, and its run to the origin; the
  degeneration to a straight ray exactly at u = v; frame corners as prints of the
  gauge-plane square, the frame-area product, and harmonic-in / arithmetic-out nesting; and
  containment of the drawing inside the viewBox for all three presets. The framing — trails as
  flow lines and frames as level curves, the origin as "the one vanishing point depth owns" — is
  a reading of the sheet, offered for the seasoning shelf, not a theorem.
