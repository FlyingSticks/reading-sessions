The 3D translations, and the means sphere born

Filed 13 September from the working record. The first sitting translated the record's 2D companions into the axonometric idiom the reader finds clearest — "down to my human bones" was the phrase — and the second sitting began with a question about sequence notation and ended with a sphere.

Landed Second sitting: the means sphere

The sitting opened with the smallest possible questions — can the terms of a sequence be any positive reals, can they be irrational, transcendental — and the answer's one restriction (positive, or the means die) turned out to be the day's hinge. Asked to find AM × HM = GM² embedded in the working diagram, the session produced the tangent construction: the circle on the two slits as diameter, its centre at the arithmetic mean, the tangent from the glass of length GM, the foot of the altitude at the harmonic mean — and then rotated it into a solid as the means sphere: the tangent revealed as a cone, touching along a whole circle, flat, transverse, standing at the harmonic mean — the neutral surface handed over as the polar plane of the glass. The identity AM × HM = GM², rotated into a solid.

Repaired The reader's catch

The first build borrowed the companion page's 0.55× depth compression, and the reader caught what that does to a sphere: it draws an ellipsoid. The revision removed all depth compression — the silhouette a true circle in every view — and added the front view and a live rate-square inset. The record notes the catch as it notes the earlier ones: the reader auditing the drawing, the checks auditing the geometry.

Flagged, then verified The sixth check

One claim on the page was flagged at delivery as the builder's own candidate, not the book's: that the arithmetic mean of the two channels' reciprocal scales equals the reciprocal scale of a pinhole at the neutral surface, at every probe depth — the algebra closing to ~4×10⁻¹⁷ over four thousand random rigs. It entered as the page's sixth check and became, in the sessions that followed, the hinge of the whole means-sphere arc: the equivalent-pinhole reading, the vergence midpoint, eventually the circle of least confusion. Filed here at its birth size: one flagged line.

Open at close of session

The three translation plates' judgment calls remained open for ruling. The means sphere joined the repository and, in the days following, gathered the annotations that became sessions 9 and 10.
