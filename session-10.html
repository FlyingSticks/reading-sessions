Answered R3, in the negative and precisely

The eye and the volume (89) closed the addendum's open item, numerics before prose: the cross-slit picture is not a perspective from the eye on the sphere or from anywhere — the imaging rays share no point, and off the seam a straight line's image bows, which no homography can undo. Found The duality, and its 1984 name

The reader conjectured a pure duality between cross-slit and two-point perspective, and the check (32, dash-named script pending upload) found it exactly: the polarity of the means sphere itself. Both slits are tangent to the sphere at its axis poles; the polarity turns each slit a quarter-turn in its own tangent plane, exchanging the rig with the dual rig; the closing law AM × HM = GM² is the pole–polar pairing of the glass with the neutral surface; E is self-conjugate — the reader stands on the duality's fixed surface; and the two-point reading is the self-dual content, which is why the horizon carries what the volume cannot. Harmonicity itself appeared as a commutation condition between two conjugacies, with a falsifiability row that breaks it and watches commutation fail. Then the reader put his own 1984 pages on the table — Glide as an Alternative to Perspective — and the session's "transparency as a relation" phrasing was superseded by the published vocabulary that had it right forty years early: actual projection, spectator projection, the always-opaque surface, the optical view from the one point where the plane dissolves, pictorial resemblance from everywhere else.
