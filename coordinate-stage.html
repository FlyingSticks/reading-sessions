The coordinate stage

At the coordinate stage the two basis points are interchangeable: any two points off a common line through O will do, and nothing distinguishes them. Naming one of them 1 is a promise about multiplication, made ahead of time.

1-line (scalars)   u-line (new direction)   z, the generic point, and its parallelogram   where u·u may land

Drag u anywhere off the 1-line. The parallelogram O, z₁, z, z₂ shears with it; the pair (x, y) does not change. No angle or length is reported, because the plane has neither yet.

Every point in the plane

z = x·1 + y·u, written z = x + yu. The parallelogram is the recipe: x steps along 1 to z₁, then y steps parallel to u, arriving at z. The same walk taken in the other order goes through z₂ = y·u. The diagonal O→z is the point/arrow double reading: z names the place reached and the walk that reaches it, and one pair (x, y) describes both.

The free decision and the one with content

1·z = z for every z in the plane. That is the free decision: it has no content to get wrong, and it is what makes the first basis point the identity. Writing z = x·1 + y·u already uses 1 both as a scalar (x of them) and as a point (the place one step out); 1 is the point along which counting and multiplying will agree.

u·u is the one decision with content. It lands on the 1-line, not the u-line, at κ·1. So the 1-line is closed under multiplication, (x·1)(y·1) = xy·1, and the u-line is not, (x·u)(y·u) = xyκ·1. On the 1-line counting and multiplying agree; u is the point along which they won't, until κ says how.

On the line O1, before any measure exists

  • 1 and −1 are swapped by the half-turn about O; O and ∞ are the half-turn's two fixed points.
  • So {−1, 1} is the harmonic pair with respect to {O, ∞}: cross-ratio −1.
  • The κ = ±1 ticks are harmonic conjugates of each other through O, and κ = 0 is the centre they are harmonic about.
  • The geometry's first sign fact is a harmonic relation on the line of scalars, before any measure exists.

Assumptions

Flat affine plane: straight lines and parallels, nothing else. No curvature, because curvature requires a measure. Not yet a ring, because there is no multiplication until κ is chosen; once it is, a field only if κ = −1.
